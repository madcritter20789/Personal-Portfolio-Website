FLIPFALL SUPPORT

Account and data deletion

App: Flipfall · Developer: Chitransh

Use this page to request deletion of your Flipfall account data or to find instructions for removing Flipfall data from Google Play Games.

Policy setup in progress. The deletion workflow and retention periods for Firebase data and support requests still need to be confirmed. This page is not yet ready for submission as a completed Google Play deletion policy.

Request account and data deletion

  1. Email chitranshnishad27@gmail.com with the subject Flipfall account and data deletion request.
  2. Describe whether you used guest play or Google Play Games cloud saves, and which data you want deleted. Include your Play Games player ID if available. Do not send passwords, authentication codes or payment card details.
  3. We will review the request and reply with any ownership verification needed, the data we can identify, and the steps required to complete deletion. A request is not confirmation that deletion has completed.
Email a deletion request

No mail app? Send the request manually to chitranshnishad27@gmail.com. You can contact us without reinstalling Flipfall.

Delete Flipfall's Play Games data

You can manage the data held by Google Play Games through Google's website:

  1. Open your Google Play Games profile and sign in with the Google account used for Flipfall.
  2. Open Your Data → Delete Play Games Account & Data.
  3. Under Delete individual game data, find Flipfall and choose Delete.

Choose the individual game entry to retain your overall Google account and Play Games profile. Google explains that deleting Play Games data does not delete data collected separately by the game developer.

Read Google's full Play Games deletion instructions.

Data covered and retention

Local guest profile and device saves
Includes saved progress, attempts, coins, power-ups, settings and generated levels stored by the app. On Android, clear Flipfall's app storage in device Settings to remove this device's app data. Device data persists until removed; clearing local storage does not delete a cloud save.
Google Play Games cloud saves
Includes the synchronized game profile: progress, coin balance, power-up inventory, settings and Endless resume data. These saves are not configured by Flipfall to expire after 90 days. Use the Play Games instructions above to manage game data, and contact us about any remaining Flipfall data.
Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics
Gameplay analytics and crash diagnostics are handled separately from Play Games saves. Their configured retention periods and the process for deleting identifiable records must be confirmed before this policy is finalized. Deleting a Play Games save does not automatically delete these records.
Deletion requests and verification messages
Requests sent by email contain your contact details and any information you provide. The retention period for these messages must be confirmed before this policy is finalized.
Google Play purchases and the Google account
Deleting Flipfall data does not delete your Google account or Google's purchase records. Google manages its own records under its policies. Deletion is not a refund request.

Loss of saved data: deleting your save may permanently remove progress, coins and power-ups, including balances from consumed coin packs. Existing device copies may upload again if you continue playing and syncing; contact support before resuming if you are completing a deletion request.
